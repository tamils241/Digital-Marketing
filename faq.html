Everything you need to know

How fast will we see results?

Paid media and CRO work usually shows signal within 2–3 weeks. SEO and organic revenue compound over 3–6 months, which is why we set that expectation up front instead of overpromising. Our median time to a first measurable win is 14 days.

Do you lock clients into long contracts?

No. Everything is month to month after a 90-day ramp. The ramp exists because anything shorter doesn't give the work enough runway to prove itself — after that, you stay as long as we're stacking wins. 30 days' notice, no exit fees.

Who actually does the work?

The same senior strategists on your call. We don't hand your account to a junior after the pitch, and we don't run your channels through a white-label team you'll never meet. Every deliverable is reviewed by someone with 8+ years in-channel.

What do you need from us to start?

Read-only ad account and analytics access, your brand assets, and one 60-minute kickoff call. That's it — we handle the rest, including the tracking rebuild if your current setup is unreliable.

Can we start with just one service?

Yes. Every plan is stackable by design. Launch covers SEO and social, and you can add paid, creative or CRO whenever the first layer is compounding. Services can also be bought à la carte as one-off projects.

What happens on the free audit call?

We review your current funnel, ad spend and search footprint live, then send back a prioritised plan within 48 hours — whether or not you hire us. No pitch deck, no pressure, and the plan is yours either way.

Is there a setup or onboarding fee?

No. Onboarding, the audit and the tracking setup are included in every plan from day one. If you want the standalone audit before committing, that's $2.5k and it's fully credited back against your first month.

What does the 90-day ramp actually mean?

The first 90 days are committed because anything shorter doesn't give the work enough runway to prove itself. After day 90 you're month to month. The 60-day growth guarantee runs alongside it.

What if it isn't working?

The 60-day growth guarantee covers this. If we haven't hit the targets we set in the first fortnight, you can walk away and keep every asset, dashboard and account structure we built. No clawbacks.

Do you manage ad spend separately?

Media budget is paid directly to Google, Meta or TikTok from your own accounts — we never mark it up. You'll always know exactly what the media working cost and what the agency earned.

How do you report results?

Scale and Dominate clients get a live dashboard with revenue, CAC, ROAS and pipeline. Every report opens with the revenue number, then the channel detail behind it. Launch clients get a written monthly report.

Who owns the creative and assets?

You do. Every file, account, dashboard and document we produce is yours, in your accounts, from day one. If we part ways, everything stays with you and we hand over access within 24 hours.

Do you work with our existing in-house team?

Often, and it's usually the best version of this. We run the channels and leave behind the systems, dashboards and playbooks so your team can take over cleanly if you want them to.

What industries do you specialise in?

We're strongest in B2B SaaS, e-commerce, fintech and health. The playbooks transfer, but we'd rather be honest that our deepest pattern recognition sits in those four.

How do you handle attribution changes?

Privacy shifts and platform tracking changes are part of the job, not an excuse. We run server-side tracking and first-party data capture from day one so iOS and consent changes degrade your numbers as little as possible.

What if I only need help with one channel?

That's exactly what the standalone audit and one-off projects are for. Plenty of clients use us for a single tracking rebuild or brand sprint and never become retainers. We're fine with that.
