Money questions

Before you commit

Is there a setup or onboarding fee?

No. Onboarding, the audit and the tracking setup are included in every plan from day one. If you want the standalone audit before committing, that's $2.5k and it's fully credited back against your first month.

What does the 90-day ramp actually mean?

The first 90 days are committed because anything shorter doesn't give the work enough runway to prove itself. After day 90 you're month to month with 30 days' notice. The 60-day growth guarantee runs alongside it.

Can I add a service mid-contract?

Yes, any time. Added services are pro-rated to your billing cycle so you're never paying twice for the same month.

Do you manage ad spend separately?

Media budget is paid directly to Google, Meta or TikTok from your own accounts — we never mark it up. You'll always know exactly what the working and the agency earned.

What if it isn't working?

The 60-day growth guarantee covers this. If we haven't hit the targets we set in the first fortnight, you can walk away and keep every asset and dashboard we built.

Is this a 12-month lock-in?

No. After the ramp it's rolling monthly. We'd rather earn the renewal every month than hide behind an annual contract.

Not sure which plan?

We'll tell you which layer to stack first

A free 30-minute call and a prioritised plan within 48 hours. If Launch is enough today, we'll say so — we only upsell when the data says it's worth it.
