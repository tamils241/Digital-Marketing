How often is the data updated?

Ad platform metrics refresh every two hours, analytics every four, and CRM data hourly. Numbers on your dashboard carry a timestamp, and the monthly close is finalised within three business days of month end — figures before that are directional.

Who can see this account?

Everyone you invite. Owners and admins see billing and campaign controls, viewers see reporting only, and your Stackly strategist sees the whole workspace. You manage everyone except your own strategist in Admin view.

Why does a campaign get paused automatically?

Two reasons: spend hits the hard cap you approved, or tracking breaks and we would rather stop than spend into a void. Paused campaigns are never left there silently — you get an alert and a recommendation within one business day.

How do I add a second ad account?

Send us the account ID from the new platform and we will connect it from our side, then confirm tracking before any spend starts. Most connections take under a day.

Can I change my budget mid-month?

Yes, and it is the right thing to do when performance shifts. Message your strategist with the new number and we will apply it in the next sync window, usually within two hours.

Do you work with our in-house team?

Most of our clients keep an in-house marketer or designer. We define who owns what in week one so nothing gets duplicated, and we hand over assets and documentation as we go.

Still stuck? Ask in the console and you will get a human answer
