NovaPay: from 9k to 37k signups

NovaPay had a product people loved and an onboarding funnel that lost two thirds of every visitor. We rebuilt the flow, then fed it with tightly segmented paid media.

  • Cut 41% of wasted ad spend in the first fortnight by consolidating to three campaign structures
  • Rebuilt onboarding to a 4-step flow — activation rate up from 38% to 71%
  • Added server-side tracking so iOS consent loss stopped under-reporting conversions by a third

Leaf & Co.: organic revenue doubled

A DTC skincare brand with great products and no organic visibility. We treated content and email as one system instead of two departments.

  • Built a comparison-content engine that now produces two ranking assets every week

CloudDesk: CAC down 54% at 3x spend

CloudDesk was scaling ads into a leaky funnel. We didn't cut budget — we fixed the layer underneath it, then turned the savings into volume.
